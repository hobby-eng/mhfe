## Review evidence

### Scope and methodology

The whole repository at the reviewed tree, by five reviewers with distinct scopes: secrets and isolation, the specification, interfaces, build and release, architecture and documents. Each file had one reader. Four skeptics then tried to refute every finding: 9 of 113 were refuted. mhfe-core 0.1.0 was read where mhfe depends on it. Coins, providers, transactions and discovery do not exist here and are not applicable.
